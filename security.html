Security Center.

Last Updated: April 2026

ORBT°360 takes the security of your business data, personal information, and virtual tour content seriously. This policy outlines the measures we take to keep your information safe.

1. Data Protection Measures

Technical Safeguards

  • All client data is stored on password-protected devices with full-disk encryption.
  • Our website uses HTTPS/SSL encryption for all data transmission.
  • Payment transactions are processed via UPI — no card data is ever stored by us.
  • Virtual tour files are uploaded directly to Google's secure infrastructure.
  • Client communications via WhatsApp are end-to-end encrypted by default.

Operational Safeguards

  • Access to client data is restricted to authorised personnel only.
  • We do not store client data on shared or public devices.
  • Physical shoot equipment is secured and insured during rental periods.
  • All shoot files are transferred and deleted from rental equipment after processing.

2. Payment Security

All payments to ORBT°360 are processed via UPI — one of India's most secure payment infrastructures, operated under NPCI guidelines.

Official UPI ID

orbt9365@hdfc.bank

  • We never ask for OTP, PIN, or card details.
  • We never send payment links via SMS from unknown numbers.
  • Always verify our UPI ID before making payment.

Fraud Warning

ORBT°360 will never ask you to share your OTP, bank PIN, or password. If you receive such a request claiming to be from ORBT°360, do not comply and report immediately to orbit360@gmail.com.

3. Virtual Tour Content Security

Your 360° virtual tour content is published on Google Maps under your Google Business Profile. Once published:

  • Content is governed by Google's Terms of Service and privacy policies.
  • Only you (as the Google Business Profile owner) can request removal from Google.
  • ORBT°360 retains shoot files for 30 days post-delivery for quality assurance only.
  • Raw shoot files are permanently deleted after 30 days unless otherwise agreed.

4. Third-Party Security

We use the following third-party services which maintain their own security standards:

Service Purpose Security Standard
Google LLC Virtual tour hosting ISO 27001, SOC 2
HDFC Bank UPI Payment processing RBI regulated, PCI-DSS
WhatsApp (Meta) Client communication End-to-end encryption

5. Incident Response

In the unlikely event of a data breach or security incident affecting your information, we will:

  • Notify affected clients within 72 hours of discovery.
  • Take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach.
  • Cooperate fully with relevant authorities as required by law.
  • Provide clear guidance on protective steps you should take.

6. Your Responsibility

To help keep your information secure, we recommend:

  • Keep your Google Business Profile login credentials confidential.
  • Verify all communications from ORBT°360 via our official contact channels.
  • Report any suspicious activity to us immediately.
  • Ensure your own devices and accounts are protected with strong passwords.

To report a security concern or suspected breach, contact us immediately.
