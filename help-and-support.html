Frequently Asked Questions

A Google 360° Virtual Tour is an immersive, interactive walkthrough of your property that appears directly on your Google Maps listing. When anyone searches for your business on Google, they can click into your listing and virtually walk through every room or zone as if they were physically there. It uses 360 degree photography stitched together seamlessly and hosted permanently by Google.

Our standard delivery is within 48 hours of the shoot. Once we upload your tour, Google processes and publishes it. This typically takes 24 to 48 hours. Express delivery (live within 24 hours) is available for an additional ₹500. We will send you the Google Maps link as soon as your tour is live.

Completely one time. You pay once and your virtual tour stays on Google permanently. There are no monthly fees, no annual renewals, and no hidden charges. Once it is live, it is live forever or until you request removal from Google yourself.

One zone = one camera placement = one 360° photograph. Think of it as any distinct area of your property: a bedroom, living room, kitchen, reception, cafe seating area, or rooftop. Each counts as one zone. A large open showroom may need only 1 to 2 zones. A 5 room homestay would typically need 5 to 7 zones. When you book, we assess your property and confirm the exact zone count before the shoot.

Yes, a Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is required to host your virtual tour. If you don't have one, we can create it for you as part of our GMB Setup service (₹1,500). If you already have one, we will link your tour directly to your existing profile.

Ensure your property is clean, well-lit (switch on all lights), and clutter-free. All areas to be photographed should be exactly as you want customers to see them. Have an authorised person present on-site during the shoot. The shoot itself typically takes 1 to 3 hours depending on property size.

Yes. Only you (as the Google Business Profile owner) can request removal or updates. To update an existing tour (e.g., after renovation), a new shoot would be required at a discounted rate for existing clients. Contact us to discuss update options.

The QR Review Stand is a physical display stand you place at your counter or reception. When a customer scans the QR code with their phone, they are taken directly to your Google review page, making it effortless for them to leave a review. More reviews = higher Google ranking = more organic customers. It is included free with our Premium/Compact packages and above.

We stand behind our work. If you are not satisfied with the quality of your virtual tour, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will re-shoot specific zones at no charge if the quality issue is on our end. We are committed to delivering a tour you are proud to show on Google Maps.
