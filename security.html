Security Center.

Last Updated: April 2026

1. On-Site & Physical Security

Mapping your physical property requires absolute trust. Our production crews operate under strict non-disclosure protocols. We do not capture restricted areas, back-office operations, or sensitive proprietary materials unless explicitly directed by the client. All hardware is monitored, and our crew operates discreetly to minimize disruption to your patrons and daily operations.

2. Digital Asset Protection

The raw 360° visual data we collect is treated as highly sensitive material:

  • Local Encryption: Raw image files are transferred to encrypted local drives immediately post-production.
  • Data Purging: To protect your structural layouts, we do not archive raw photography indefinitely. All raw, unstitched files are permanently purged from our local systems 30 days after the live Google Maps link is successfully delivered.

3. Platform Infrastructure

ORBT°360 relies on Google's enterprise-grade infrastructure. The final digital twins and virtual tours are hosted directly on Google Cloud servers via the Google Maps ecosystem. This ensures your tours benefit from Google's 99.9% uptime, redundant backups, and world-class cybersecurity defenses against DDoS and scraping attacks.

4. Financial & Transaction Security

We have eliminated third-party payment gateways to reduce your risk footprint. All financial transactions are processed strictly via India's UPI (Unified Payments Interface) directly to our HDFC banking partner.

We never ask for, process, or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or banking passwords. Confirmations are handled via end-to-end encrypted WhatsApp channels.

5. Report a Vulnerability

Security is an evolving standard. If you believe you have found a vulnerability in our booking flow, or if you spot an unblurred sensitive detail (like a license plate or face) in your published tour, please alert our security desk immediately for expedited removal.
