Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

At ORBT°360, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data of our clients and their patrons. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you engage our virtual tour photography and Google My Business (GMB) integration services.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect information necessary to execute our services effectively:

  • Client Information: Business name, address, contact numbers, and email addresses provided during the booking process.
  • Payment Data: UPI transaction IDs and payment screenshots sent via WhatsApp for reservation confirmation. We do not store direct bank account details.
  • Visual Data: 360° panoramic imagery of your physical business premises.

3. Handling of Visual Data & Anonymity

Our primary service involves capturing immersive imagery of your commercial space. To comply with Google Maps Street View privacy standards, our post-production team actively blurs recognizable human faces and vehicle license plates captured inadvertently during the shoot. ORBT°360 retains the raw footage securely for a period of 30 days post-delivery to facilitate any necessary revisions, after which raw files may be permanently deleted.

4. Third-Party Integrations

We act as a bridge between your business and Google. The virtual tours and GMB setups we finalize are uploaded directly to Google's servers. Once published, this visual data is governed by Google’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. ORBT°360 does not sell, rent, or trade your contact information to external marketing agencies or unauthorized third parties.

5. Communications

By initiating a booking via WhatsApp or providing your contact details, you consent to receive transactional communications regarding scheduling, SLA updates, and delivery links. We respect your inbox and strictly prohibit spam.
